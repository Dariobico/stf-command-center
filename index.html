01 / Mission

OUR MISSION

The Special Task Force exists to provide a highly disciplined, professional and accountable response to situations requiring specialised operational capability.

Every STF member represents more than themselves. Every action reflects upon the unit, its leadership and the wider UNITY REJECTS Government.

Our objective is simple: protect life, uphold the law, maintain operational discipline and complete every assignment with professionalism.

THE STF STANDARD

"We do not seek recognition. We seek results. We do not act for ourselves. We act for those we serve. We remain disciplined when others lose control."

02 / Foundation

MORALS & VALUES

The standards expected from every member of the Special Task Force.

01

INTEGRITY

05 / Doctrine

STF DOCTRINE

01

CHAIN OF COMMAND

Every operator understands who they report to and who holds operational authority.

02

COMMUNICATION

Clear, concise and professional communication is required before, during and after operations.

03

OPERATIONAL CONTROL

Operators maintain awareness, follow briefings and avoid unnecessary escalation.

04

PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

STF personnel remain professional while representing the unit both on and off duty.

05

ACCOUNTABILITY

Operational decisions are documented and members may be required to explain their actions.

06

MISSION FIRST

Personal ego never takes priority over the safety, objectives and reputation of the unit.

07 / Standards

CODE OF CONDUCT

EVERY STF MEMBER SHALL

  • Follow the chain of command.
  • Remain professional while on duty.
  • Follow lawful orders.
  • Communicate clearly with other units.
  • Protect the reputation of the STF.
  • Respect civilians and fellow officers.
  • Accept responsibility for their actions.
  • Report serious misconduct through command.

ZERO-TOLERANCE CONDUCT

  • Abuse of STF authority.
  • Deliberate corruption or dishonesty.
  • Unauthorised disclosure of operational information.
  • Disrespect toward command or colleagues.
  • Reckless behaviour that endangers others.
  • Using STF status for personal benefit.
  • Ignoring legitimate command instructions.
  • Conduct that damages the unit's reputation.
08 / Operations

OPERATIONAL READINESS

BEFORE DEPLOYMENT

Every operation should begin with a clear objective, briefing, assignment of responsibilities and confirmation of the chain of command.

AFTER DEPLOYMENT

Operators complete a debrief, report significant events and identify lessons that can improve future operations.
